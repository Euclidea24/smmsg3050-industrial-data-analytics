## Q&A preparation

1. Why this dataset and not a larger one?
2. How did you prevent leakage?
3. Why this metric?
4. How sensitive is the result to the split and seed?
5. Why not a simpler / more complex model?
6. What would you do with one more week?
7. Which result is most likely to be wrong?
8. How would this be deployed?
9. What is the business impact?
10. What did you learn that generalizes?
